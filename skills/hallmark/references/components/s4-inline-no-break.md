### S4 · Inline (no break)
The heading is a heavier phrase inside the paragraph's first line, never on its own line: `craft.md` § 7 item 3. A `<span class="head-inline">` at weight 600 or 700 in the body face and ink; the paragraph keeps its measure and nothing collapses on mobile.
*Don't confuse with:* S2 Hanging (which separates with air).
