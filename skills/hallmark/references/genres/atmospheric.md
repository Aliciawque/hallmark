# Genre: atmospheric

The AI-creative and after-dark product page: generative music, video, image, voice, model playgrounds, anything the brief calls moody, cinematic or nocturnal. A canvas first, then a tool. The theme deal filters the catalog by this genre, and it holds the genre's light-paper exceptions too.

## Voice

- **Display:** a weighty sans or a roman classical serif, plain English, tight tracking. Never italic.
- **Body:** the same family at 400, light ink on dark paper.
- **Accent:** one warm hue, used in the canvas treatment, on focus rings and on small tags; never on display text.
- **Layout:** a centred hero is the normal answer when the type is the visual (a Poster or Banner posture); with a visual, it stands beside the text (44c). The canvas is the design and the type sits on it.
- **Motion:** fade only; the atmosphere does the work.
- **Copy:** direct, slightly poetic, specific: *Make a house song about quitting your job.*

## Allowed here

At most two fixed radial blooms on the body, 20 to 30% of the canvas each, no animation (gate 29 is loosened this far and no further); a centred hero (gate 6b loosened); pill controls with an accent fill; elevated cards by lightness step (`--color-paper-2`, `-3`) instead of hairlines; display up to 6rem.

## Refused here

A coloured glow shadow, a radial spotlight behind the hero, or a mesh gradient (the bloom is the one licence); light sections sneaking into a dark build; italic heads; a third hue; glass; gradient text.

## Chrome that fits

A floating pill on a blur backdrop, an edge-aligned bar, a command palette, a statement footer. Dense link furniture fights the register. Affinities, not assignments.

Stamp: `genre: atmospheric`.
