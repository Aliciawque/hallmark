# Genre: editorial (the default)

Content-led briefs: portfolios, manifestos, type specimens, agency sites, magazine pieces, podcasts, brand stories, considered consumer marketing. The silent default when no other genre signal fires. The theme deal filters the catalog by this genre; nothing here lists themes.

## Voice

- **Display:** a roman serif, a condensed sans or a display-heavy face, committed to an extreme weight (300 or 700 plus). Never a default sans.
- **Body:** a workhorse serif or a plain non-default sans at 45 to 75ch.
- **Accent:** one warm or cool hue under 5% of any viewport.
- **Layout:** asymmetric; hairlines, not card borders; generous air.
- **Motion:** quiet, one orchestrated entrance.
- **Copy:** specific, hand-set, slightly literary; verbs over adjectives.

## Allowed here

Hairline rules, fleurons, drop caps, double rules; italic inside running body copy; asymmetric column ratios (2:5, 3:7); hand-built SVG and pure CSS art; edge-aligned headlines; a single `<mark>` band behind the x-height.

## Refused here

Gradient-filled pills; a centred-everything hero (the genre is left-biased or asymmetric, unless the theme's material is a centred bill); glass (the medium is paper); pure black or white paper and ink.

## Chrome that fits

A masthead, a quiet edge-aligned bar, a colophon, a letter close. A marquee footer or a floating blur pill fights the register. Affinities, not assignments: gate 42 only asks that a default be named in the stamp rather than arrived at by reflex.

Stamp: `genre: editorial`.
