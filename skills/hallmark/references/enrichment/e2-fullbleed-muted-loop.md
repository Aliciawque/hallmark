### E2 · Demo video, full-bleed muted loop with a ghost overlay

Video fills the fold, ghosted through `mix-blend-mode: multiply` over a paper-coloured overlay so the type stays readable. The video is wallpaper, not subject.

*Use when:* the product's feel is the message (mood, tactility, atmosphere).
*Avoid when:* the product needs to be seen clearly; use E1 or E4 instead.

**Knobs:**
- Ghost opacity (0.3 · 0.5 · 0.7)
- Text alignment (left-bias · centred)
- Out-of-viewport behaviour (keep looping · pause). Hover and focus always pause it (18).

**Example.** A small fashion label's spring lookbook. An eight-second muted loop of fabric draping in a studio, multiplied over a 0.5-opacity warm-cream overlay so the display headline ("Lookbook four, spring 2026.") reads over the moving footage. Pauses on hover and focus so the lede can be read; a VTT caption track describes the footage.
