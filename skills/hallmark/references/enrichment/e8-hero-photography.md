### E8 · Hero photography, one tightly cropped image

The cookbook's H6 archetype, cross-referenced here. Knobs are in [`component-cookbook.md`](../component-cookbook.md).

**Example.** A small Lisbon café. One tightly cropped photograph of the espresso machine at dawn, 4/3, not full-bleed. The caption sits margin-aligned at lower left in the body face, a step smaller, sentence case ("The espresso machine at 6:42 a.m., plate four."). The photograph is desaturated 8% from the source to sit with the page's warm paper. Pair photography with a tone-matched type pairing ([`craft.md` § 1](../craft.md)); a luxury-tone photo on a brutalist page jars.
