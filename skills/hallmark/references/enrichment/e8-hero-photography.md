### E8 · Hero photography

One photograph beside the headline (the photograph visual of [`components/h2-split-diptych.md`](../components/h2-split-diptych.md)); the full-bleed version is [`components/h6-photographic-fold.md`](../components/h6-photographic-fold.md).

- **Source.** The user's own photograph. Without one, a `data-slot` sized for it with the theme's placeholder; never a stock person, never a face as social proof (F9). Sources and licences when the brief allows stock scenery: [`assets.md`](../assets.md) § Photography.
- **Crop.** Tighter than feels safe: the object, not the room. 4/3 or 1/1 beside text; the subject's weight sits toward the headline.
- **Tone.** Pull it toward the paper: desaturate 5 to 10% and warm or cool it to the paper's hue so the page and the picture share light. Never a gradient wash over it (29).
- **Caption.** A `<figcaption>` beneath or in the margin, in the body face a step smaller, sentence case, stating a fact ("The espresso machine at 6:42, Rua da Rosa"). No credit line under a placeholder (66).
- **Weight.** `fetchpriority="high"` and explicit `width` and `height` on the LCP image; never `loading="lazy"` on it (28a).

**Example.** A small Lisbon café. One photograph of the espresso machine at dawn, 4/3, cropped to the group head and the steam; desaturated 8% toward the warm paper; the caption beneath names the machine, the time and the street.
