From hum to band

  1. You hum

    A verse, a chorus, the riff that keeps coming back. Your phone is enough.

  2. Driftsong listens

    It finds the notes, the key and the tempo in your hum.

  3. The band arrives

    Keys, bass, drums and strings, written around your melody, never over it.
