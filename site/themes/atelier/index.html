How a visit goes

By appointment, so the room is yours while you choose.

  1. Ask for a day

    Any open day, through the form below.

  2. Hear back with a time

    The reply names the hour and the street.

  3. Smell all four

    Side by side, with nobody waiting behind you.

  4. Leave with one, or none

    Or come back to decide.
