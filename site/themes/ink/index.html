How borrowing works

Four steps, and a Saturday at each end.

  1. Join

    One membership covers the whole year.

  2. Come on a Saturday

    Pick things up while the doors are open.

  3. Take home what’s in

    Anything tagged In can go home with you.

  4. Bring it back

    Return it on a Saturday for the next neighbour.
